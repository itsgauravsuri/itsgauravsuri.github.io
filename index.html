How I work

A loop of watching the numbers, putting budget where it works, and keeping leads moving through to booking.

  1. Monitor and analyze

    Campaign monitoring and performance analysis.

  2. Plan the budget

    Test new destinations, then allocate spend based on results.

  3. Improve lead quality

    Review lead quality and work on conversion.

  4. Track in the CRM

    Lead status and follow-up monitoring.

  5. Align the teams

    Keep content and sales working from the same picture.

With the content team

I share campaign requirements and coordinate destination-specific ad creatives.

With the sales team

I guide hot-lead prioritization, lead nurturing and follow-up strategies.

With the numbers

I track ad spend, ROAS and lead quality, and use them to decide where budget goes.
